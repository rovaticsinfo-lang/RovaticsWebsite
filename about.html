About Rovatics

Turning human effort into robotic capability.

Rovatics is building a robotics engineering company focused on solving physical-world problems with practical automation, intelligent robots, and humanoid systems.

Our mission

Our mission is simple: identify work that currently depends on human physical effort and engineer robotic systems that can perform that work reliably in the real world.

We believe the right answer is not always a humanoid. Some problems are best solved with industrial arms, mobile robots, specialized machines, autonomous vehicles, or combinations of systems. We select the architecture around the requirement.

Where humanoids are the right fit, we focus on the intelligence, simulation, perception, planning, control, and validation needed to make them useful in human-centered environments.

What guides us
A

Problem before platform

Start with the real workflow and outcome. Choose the robot after understanding the job.

B

Simulation before scale

Use large-scale virtual testing to expose failures and compare solutions before extensive physical trials.

C

Engineering over hype

Measure task success, reliability, cycle time, safety, intervention rate, and operating cost.

Where we can help

From factories to human-designed environments.

Our scope is any physical workflow where robotics can create a meaningful improvement in effort, consistency, safety, throughput, or availability.

▦

Factories & production

Material movement, machine tending, assembly support, inspection, repetitive handling, and other high-frequency operations.

◈

Warehouses & logistics

Picking, transport, loading, sorting, replenishment, and repetitive movement across structured or changing environments.

⌁

Facilities & field work

Inspection, maintenance support, monitoring, handling, and tasks that require robots to operate beyond a fixed production cell.

◎

Human-centered environments

Explore humanoid and mobile manipulation where existing spaces, tools, and workflows were designed for people.
