Our purpose

Every physical problem deserves a robotic solution.

We start with the work, not with a particular robot. We understand the task, constraints, environment, human effort, and required outcome — then determine what kind of automation makes sense.

⌘

Industrial automation

Automate repetitive production, material handling, inspection, machine tending, warehouse movement, and other factory workflows where consistency and throughput matter.

✧

Humanoid & general-purpose work

Explore humanoid robots for environments designed around people — especially where stairs, tools, workstations, objects, and existing infrastructure make fixed automation difficult.

⟲

Physical-world problem solving

For logistics, facilities, field operations, inspection, handling, and other sectors, we engineer systems around the real task and the level of human intervention you want to remove.

How Rovatics works

We don't just build a robot. We build the complete path to reliable automation.

Our work connects task analysis, robot selection, simulation, AI, control, testing, and deployment. The objective is measurable reduction of manual effort while maintaining appropriate safety and operational oversight.
