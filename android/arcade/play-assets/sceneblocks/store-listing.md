## Full description (4000 max)
```
Drop the shapes. Clear the lines. Watch the world wake up.

Rovatics Scene Blocks is a calm, colourful block puzzle with 1000 levels. You get three shapes at a time, place them on an 8x8 grid, and every full row or column clears with a burst of colour. The twist: you can ROTATE every shape with a tap, so there is always a clever fit waiting to be found.

🧱 PUZZLE YOUR WAY
• 1000 levels, each with a clear goal: clear a number of lines, or reach a score
• A move limit on every level, so every placement matters
• Tap a shape to rotate it, or tap with a second finger while you drag
• A glow shows which lines a move will clear before you let go
• Combos, perfect clears and 1 to 3 stars on every level

🌅 WORLDS THAT COME ALIVE
• Live Sky: the scene follows your real clock, from sunrise to starry night
• Six animated worlds: space, ocean, forest, candy land, neon city and snow peaks
• Journey mode carries you through a new world every 10 levels
• Prefer one look? Lock any scene you like with a single tap

🎉 MADE TO FEEL GREAT
• Particles, screen shake and cheerful combo call-outs
• Sound effects and haptic feedback (switch sound off any time)
• Your progress and stars are saved on your device

✅ FREE AND PRIVATE
• No ads, no account, no in-app purchases
• Works fully offline
• No camera, microphone, location or contacts access

Easy to learn, hard to put down. How many stars can you collect?
```
