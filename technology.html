Our engineering loop

More simulation. Fewer surprises in the real world.

Physical testing is essential, but it is expensive and slow to scale. We use simulation to multiply experiments, discover failure modes, and narrow the solution space before committing to large physical trials.

01

Model reality

Represent the robot, environment, sensors, objects, physics, task and constraints as faithfully as practical.

02

Generate variation

Change positions, objects, timing, lighting, dynamics, layouts, and other conditions to test robustness.

03

Run & compare

Execute many trials, measure outcomes, identify failures, and compare candidate approaches.

04

Validate in reality

Take proven behaviours to physical systems through staged testing and monitored deployment.

What success means

Automation should create measurable operational value.

Depending on the task, we can evaluate success through task completion, cycle time, intervention frequency, reliability, safety boundaries, throughput, consistency, and operating cost.

Less manual intervention

Reduce the number of routine physical actions that require a person to be present or continuously involved.

Higher consistency

Repeat defined operations with measurable performance and controlled variation.

Better scalability

Use simulation and reusable software workflows to accelerate development across tasks, environments, and robot platforms.

Our principle: We do not assume every problem needs a humanoid. We determine the required capability first, then engineer the most appropriate robotic solution and validate it against the real operating conditions.
