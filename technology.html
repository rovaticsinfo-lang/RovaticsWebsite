Robotics development

Intelligent solutions for the physical world.

We help shape and develop robotic systems around your goals — from understanding the task and choosing an approach to simulation, testing, and validation.

⌕

1. Understand the challenge

We explore the task, environment, constraints, and desired outcome to identify what the robotic system needs to do.

Discover · Define · Plan

⌘

2. Robot & system selection

Match the task to industrial robots, cobots, mobile manipulators, autonomous platforms, humanoids, custom mechanisms, or hybrid systems.

Right robot for the job

▦

3. High-scale simulation

Create digital environments and run large numbers of virtual trials to test motion, perception, contact, planning, control, and task completion.

Thousands of scenarios

✳

4. AI, control & learning

Develop the intelligence required for the task — perception, planning, learned policies, manipulation, navigation, feedback control, and decision-making.

Perceive · Plan · Act

◉

5. Stress testing & evaluation

Test variations, edge cases, environmental changes, failures, recovery behaviour, and operational limits before physical deployment.

Fail early · Improve faster

↗

6. Physical validation & deployment

Move from simulation to controlled hardware trials, measure real performance, refine the system, and establish the level of human oversight required in operation.

Sim-to-real · Deploy · Monitor
