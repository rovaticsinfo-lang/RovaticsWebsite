🏁 Rovatics Racers

You race against two computer cars. Steer around the cones, avoid the puddles, and roll over the ⚡ pads for a speed boost. Five levels, and the rivals get faster and smarter each time!

Pick your car and a level. Finish in the top 2 to unlock the next level! On a tablet or phone, use the big buttons under the track.
